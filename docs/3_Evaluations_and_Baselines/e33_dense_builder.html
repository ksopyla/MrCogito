How the numbers are made

  • Parameters are exact formulas of the modules in nn/perceiver_ar_lm.py and nn/latent_memory.py. Checked against evaluation/text_checks.count_params on 24 Oct: dense 30,293,140 and 100,966,686; E33 29,946,402 and 100,965,425. Edits outside today's code are still counted the same way.
  • Compute counts multiply-adds (2 FLOP each) of every projection, attention score and value read, per token, averaged over the row; training = 3 × forward. Parts with frozen weights (the naming lens) count 2 ×, parts without gradient (the order term's rank count) 1 ×. Kernel overheads, masks and recomputation are not counted.
  • Speed is anchored to measured rates on one RTX 3090 at 4k rows: dense_plain 30M ≈ 60k tokens/s, e33m_plain 30M ≈ 20k (19.8–22.2k, 19 Oct). A build is scaled by its FLOP count from its own family's anchor. E33 is about 3 × slower for about 1.2 × the FLOPs, so most of its extra time is overhead the count does not see (attention masks, the fp32 rank count, half the micro-batch). Treat new numbers as ±30 %.
  • Loss sizes at step 0 assume uniform predictions and uniform reads at initialization. They show how loud each term starts, not how it trains.
