Length battery: does it hold up as the input grows?

Each chart is one exam and training stage. The model trains at the length in the subtitle, then reads longer inputs with no more training. The y axis is first-letter accuracy, the honest score when the book holds several candidate answers. Higher is better; the dashed line is the 75 % pass mark and chance is 25 %. Hollow points are the mean over answer letters, shown unconnected where the first letter was not measured (it overstates multi-candidate exams). Lines join the first-letter medians over seeds.
