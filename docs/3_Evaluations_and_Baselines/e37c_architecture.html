Two agents in one training row

Yesterday's test showed that a trained 100M model, split into a reader (agent A) and an answerer (agent B), loses most of its score when B gets only A's memory: 43 % of needles found at 16k tokens against 97 % for one agent. The memory holds the answer; B fails because it starts cold, a situation the model never met in training. E37c trains on that situation: every row is cut at random points, and the text after a cut may use its nearby-words layers only on its own side.

What crosses a cut, and what does not
CrossesDoes not cross
The reading layer's memory of every earlier token: one key and one value per token and loop pass (512 numbers per token). From it the model also builds its concepts (64 per block of 512 tokens) and their summaries. The local layers' caches of the tokens before the cut (13 local-layer calls × 128 numbers per token). These are what yesterday's "plus A's last 16 tokens" condition added back.
Positions keep counting through the cut. That is a number, not content. Raw text. There are no n-gram input tables in these models; the model refuses cut rows if they are on.

4 · Information budget (16k-token document, number needle)

Bars are on a log scale. The answer needs about 23 bits. The memory is thousands of times larger than the document's information, so storage is not the bottleneck. The expected bottleneck is the address: B must form a good question to the memory from its own few tokens, with empty nearby-words layers. Measured yesterday: with the same memory, B finds 43 % cold and 93 % when it also gets A's last 16 tokens' local caches.

5 · The run and its pass marks

ItemChoice
ModelThe concept-read 100M model (162.4M parameters), from its 16k-stage final weights. No new parameters.
DataPlain long documents (LongDocs-lite), rows of 16,384 tokens, one document per row. 0.2B tokens = 12,207 rows.
CutsEvery row. The gap between cuts is drawn between 32 and 4,096 tokens (short gaps as likely as long ones on a log scale): about 20 cuts per row, about 15 % of tokens within 128 tokens after a cut.
Recipe32 rows a step, 381 steps, learning rate 6.4e-5 falling to 6.4e-6 (where the 16k stage stopped). One seed.
MachineOne L40S on AWS (spot). About 4.2 h training + 1.5 h exams, about $11. Hard limit 9 h; it powers itself off.

Also required: with no memory, or another document's memory, B stays at ≤ 5 % (so the gain is real reading); the tuned model as one agent loses at most 7 points, and its loss on long documents without cuts rises by at most 0.01. If the stop mark is hit, the hand-over keeps A's last 32–64 tokens' local caches (0.1–0.2 MB), which already gives 95–100 % with no training.
