Multi-hop on real text, taught by written reasoning, at 100M

The bet: a 100M notebook model is trained on real multi-hop questions and maths whose answers write their reasoning out. A fixed rule reads each step's word and location off that text and supervises one loop round per step. The written steps are then removed in three stages. At exam time it should answer directly, as well as a dense model trained the same way at 4k, and stay accurate at 16k–64k, where the dense model's window cannot see the evidence.

The 30M synthetic pilot (R2) reached compose 92 % and deduce 99 % at 4k this way, with no generator labels. Spec: docs/experiments_specs/ahead/E33s_written_chains_real_data.md.

Leaks this prevents

The written chain comes after Answer:. The loop rounds run at the Answer: position, so causally they cannot see the chain in any stage. The chain only provides targets, and in stage 1 next-token text to predict.

Exam documents never contain a chain. The evidence-removed twin swaps the supporting paragraphs for distractors, so any score left is the floor from guessing or from what the model memorised.

Dev and test questions never enter training. Distractor padding for the exams comes from the dev pool only.

Information budget along the path

Rough figures for one 4k row, to show where the bottleneck should be. A token of a 16k vocabulary carries at most 14 bits. The board's measured reader ceiling is about 85 bits per note vector.

Capacity is not the expected limit: one note holds a hop's entity. Selection is: the round must land on the right note among thousands. The per-round read target and the naming loss train exactly that. The pilot's reads landed on the right fact 93–96 % of the time.

How we will know

Success (stage 3, answers only)

No harm at 4k. MuSiQue and HotpotQA F1 within 2 points of the dense model, or above.

The latent chain. MuSiQue F1 at 4k at least 70 % of the ceiling, the dense model writing its chain out. At least 5 F1 above the same model trained without chains.

Length. At 16k and 64k it keeps at least 80 % of its own 4k F1. The dense model, which sees only its last 4k tokens, sits within 5 F1 of its no-evidence floor.

Maths, secondary. GSM8K exact answers are reported for all arms. No gate if all are below 5 %.

Stop early if

The rule parses fewer than 60 % of the training chains (CPU check, before any GPU).

After pretraining, the notebook model's language loss is worse than dense by more than 0.10 nats.

After stage 3, MuSiQue F1 at 4k is below the no-evidence floor + 5.

Written chains add less than 1 F1 over the no-chain control.

Cost: about 280 GPU-hours, roughly 4 days on Polonez and Odra. A 30M pipeline pilot of about 8 GPU-hours runs first.
