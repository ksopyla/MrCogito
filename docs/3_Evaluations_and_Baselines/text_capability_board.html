Calibration ladder at a glance

The dense reference alone, growing the token budget and then the model, until it passes the gating tasks at the 4k training length — only then can a round compare architectures. Times are UTC. Details of each finished run are under Results.

Results

One block per round. Every model in a round trains from scratch on the same data, with the same number of tokens and the same compute cap; each gets its own tuned step size. Scores are exact answers, word for word including the final period, at the training length; a pass is 75 % or more.

Length: does it still work on longer texts?

Each model trains at the length marked by the vertical line, then reads longer documents with no more training (more story filler, the same facts). Dashed grey: the 75 % pass mark. Dotted red: the guessing rate.

Learning curves: when does each skill appear?

Score at the training length on checkpoints saved along training (result rounds: 10, 25, 50, 75 and 100 % of the training tokens; calibration runs: every 10 or 20 %), and the held-out loss of the training mix (lower is better).

Task guide

Each question comes at the end of a document of simple stories about an invented cast, with fact sentences woven in. Names and stories in the exams never appear in training.

Calibration and pipeline checks: not results

Work that sets up or checks the protocol, kept apart from the results. Pipeline checks are tiny models (2M parameters, a quarter of a million tokens, minutes) that show training, scoring and resuming work end to end; their scores mean nothing. Step-size tuning runs pick each model's step size by held-out loss, never by exam scores.
