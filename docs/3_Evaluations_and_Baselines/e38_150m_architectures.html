The 150M family: one body, four ways to read the past

Four models trained on the same 11.5B tokens, in the same order, with the same 13 layers. Twelve of the layers only ever see the last 128 tokens. One layer, the reading layer, is allowed to look further back, and it is the only thing that differs between the four. Three of them read the past through a different kind of notebook; the fourth rereads every earlier word and is the ceiling inside the 64k-token training length.

1 · The whole network at a glance

One row of 16,384 tokens in training; width 896 everywhere in the body. "local" = sliding-window attention over the last 128 tokens. The loop runs the same two layers four times with the same weights; only a small learned "pass number" vector is added before each pass.
