## Interpretation

**Dense hops wall sits between seq=256 (S0 PASS) and seq=512 (S0 FAIL).** Do not
invent seq=192.

At seq=256, exclusive identity does **not** compose hops (0 bits) even though
dense recovers 24 of 26 bits. Uncompressed E18 is also **0 bits** — this is not
an E21-only compression failure. Tiny seq=128 had E18 97.5% and an E21 hops wall
(~10 bits / 49%). Stretching the same 26-bit DFA to 256 kills both global-read
arms while dense still solves it.

## Decision

Keep the spec in `ahead/`. Do not extra-step E21. Do not seq=192. Do not 4k.
Do not Glyph. GPU hops vs dense: **FAIL at 256** (E18 and E21 0 bits); dense
unsolvable at 512. Next ONE experiment: **stop hops extra-steps** — identity
already does INDEX/MATCH/SELECT at 512; hops does not compose even when dense
can. Architecture change if hops is still the question, not a smaller seq.
