Teacher-forced concept use and free-run health are **not the same axis**. Opening writes with init 0.3 on shared depth restores / preserves causal-use and lowers CE, but free-run stays E16b-broken (`real ≪ zero`, digit loops). Per-layer + cold init does the opposite on free-run (healthier decode) without engaging writes. The next bet should open writes **without** the shared free-run poison — per-layer + moderate init.

## Decision

- Treat shared init-0.3 @1B as a **control result**: open gates ≠ free-run fix on shared topology.
- Do **not** jump straight to per-layer + 0.3 as “more of what worked at 100M” — 0.3 is aggressive and already failed free-run on shared at full budget.
- Prefer a **middle** write init on per-layer banks (`WRITE_GATE_INIT=0.1`, tanh≈0.1 = the open-gate threshold) as E17b — enough to escape the 0.01 cold-start, less than the 0.3 regime that coexists with free-run collapse on shared.
