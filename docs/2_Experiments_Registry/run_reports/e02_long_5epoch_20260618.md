## Interpretation

Longer training on prefix→suffix delivers **modest STS-B gains** (0.702 → 0.714) but a **large
geometry gain** that the upgraded metrics now make visible: RankMe 246, anisotropy 0.32, cosine
0.124 — this is the healthiest concept geometry the project has produced. Critically, prefix→suffix
concepts **de-collapse with more training**, the inverse of reconstruction's late-epoch collapse.
This reframes "concept collapse" from a universal failure into an **objective-dependent** one:
reconstruction + strong AR decoder collapses; prefix→suffix does not, because the decoder cannot
bypass the bottleneck from its own gold left context.

The STS-B plateau (0.70 → 0.71 despite 5× the training and much richer geometry) suggests zero-shot
mean-pooled STS-B is near-saturated for this architecture/size. The Tier-2.5 probe supports that
read: attention pooling recovers a large positive delta on SICK relatedness (Pearson +0.336 over
mean), but the absolute SICK score remains weak and PAWS is mixed. So E02-long's geometry is real
and partly distributed across slots, but a simple readout still does not turn it into broadly strong
pair-task utility.

---

## Decision

**Verdict: positive.** Confirms prefix→suffix as the better semantic objective and shows it
de-collapses with scale. The Tier-2.5 probe adds an important nuance: distributed concept information
is present (SICK attention ≫ mean), but readout utility is not yet broadly strong (PAWS mixed).
Decision: treat prefix→suffix, not reconstruction, as the default objective for the next architecture
experiment. The highest-value next run is E05 from scratch with prefix→suffix, using the windowed
decoder to make concepts the only cross-window memory; the frozen-teacher anchor remains auxiliary.
