Every conclusion drawn from these runs, with its state: holds (repeated, or overwhelming within its runs), single run (needs a second), open (cannot be tested yet), retracted (disproved, kept with the date).

Scoreboard: who reads which facts

The current comparison: 30M models, plain input, 1.5B training tokens, exams v2. Each cell is the share of exact answers at the 4k training length (one number per run), with the facts placed near the end, the score at 16k (four times the training length) and the score on rephrased facts underneath. Green border: a pass (75 % or more). Shaded: learned (clearly above the floor). Grey: not learned. The reading rules are under the table.

Length: does it still work on longer texts?

Each model trains at the length marked by the vertical line, then reads longer documents with no more training (more story filler, the same facts). Dashed grey: the 75 % pass mark. Dotted red: the floor (guessing; latest: 1 in 5). Dense has no method for reading past its training length, so its scores there are not a memory comparison.

Learning curves: when does each skill appear?

Score at the training length on checkpoints saved along training (result rounds: 10, 25, 50, 75 and 100 % of the training tokens; calibration runs: every 10 or 20 %), and the held-out loss of the training mix (lower is better).

Task guide

Each question comes at the end of a document of simple stories about an invented cast, with fact sentences woven in. Names and stories in the exams never appear in training.

All runs, in order

Everything trained so far and what is queued, oldest first, each with its standing: in use (feeds the scoreboard), calibration (the dense reference alone: sets the recipe and budget), history (older exams or recipe: kept, not compared with current runs) and why. Protocol log: what changed, and what it did to earlier results

Every change to the exams, the training recipe or the reading rules. "Re-read" means the stored measurements were read again with the new rules, with no retraining; "history" means the earlier runs used other exams or another recipe and are kept for the record, not compared.

Run details

One block per run, newest first; click to open. The filters of "All runs" apply here too. Every model in a run trains from scratch on the same data, with the same number of tokens and compute cap, and its own tuned step size. Scores are exact answers, word for word including the final period; a pass is 75 % or more.

Calibration and pipeline checks: not results

Work that sets up or checks the protocol, kept apart from the results. Pipeline checks are tiny models (2M parameters, a quarter of a million tokens, minutes) that show training, scoring and resuming work end to end; their scores mean nothing. Step-size tuning runs pick each model's step size by held-out loss, never by exam scores.
