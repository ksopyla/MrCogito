    What changed the results

    Every change of recipe, architecture, exam or reading rule whose effect we measured, newest first: before (hollow) → after (filled). "Measurement" changes the exam or the rule, not the model.

    What we know

    Every conclusion with how sure we are. Click one for its evidence. A claim is never deleted: when the evidence changes, its state does.

    Tasks and exam versions

    Every exam is a document of simple stories about invented people, with fact sentences woven in and a question at the end. Names and stories in the exams never appear in training.
