### Data efficiency

Say how efficiency is measured before claiming it. Three measures, strongest first:
1. **Same budget:** a reference's published checkpoint at our token count, same harness (e.g. Pythia every 2.1B tokens).
2. **Tokens to match:** how many tokens the reference needs to reach our score (log-linear between its checkpoints),
   as a multiple of ours.
3. **Against the field (quoted):** our score against the training tokens other entries report (a board snapshot).
State what the comparison cannot separate: data quality vs architecture. The architecture's own share needs a
same-data control (a full-attention model on the same mix).
